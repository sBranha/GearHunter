GEAR HUNTER v0.10.6 - FARM ROUTE

OPEN: HUNT LIST > PLAN FARM ROUTE or /gh route

1. Add gear targets in HUNT LIST and leave the targets unfinished.
2. Index the Blizzard Adventure Guide in GEAR SEARCH to identify possible dungeon/raid sources.
3. PLAN FARM ROUTE groups your unfinished targets by exact Adventure Guide instance ID.
4. Observed merchant locations are also included, labeled LAST SEEN VENDOR (not verified).
5. Select a stop; select an item to inspect the boss. Use SHOW ENTRANCE / SHOW BOSS / OPEN GUIDE.
6. MOVE UP / MOVE DOWN changes the order of destination stops. NEXT STOP advances through the list.
7. RETURN TO HUNT LIST to check items off; completed items leave the route automatically.

IMPORTANT: The route is manually ordered and does not provide travel times or automatic directions. Missing item sources are listed as NEED SOURCES. When Blizzard has no reliable entrance/boss coordinates, the addon does not invent a map pin.
