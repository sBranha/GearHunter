GEAR HUNTER v0.11.2 - UPGRADE PLANNER

Open /gh plan or MY GEAR -> OPEN GEAR UPGRADE PLANNER.
Select any worn piece, compare its live rank and cap, and flag it as a priority.
At a Blizzard item upgrade NPC, select that same item and press REFRESH COSTS.
Only the costs actually returned by the game will be shown.
No guessed currency prices, automatic upgrades, or automatic spending.
The selected item cost preview is session-only; saved priorities are per character.
