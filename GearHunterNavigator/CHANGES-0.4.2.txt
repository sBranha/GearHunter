GEAR HUNTER NAVIGATOR v0.4.2 — CLASS-AWARE LEVELING

NEW
- Leveling tab now reads class, level, specialization, faction, current zone and Blizzard's active quest log.
- For a level 4 Alliance Warlock in Stormwind, offers starter-zone guidance and early Warlock combat tips.
- Displays a relevant active quest (or completed turn-in) when the log supplies level information.
- Won't suggest a quest more than 5 character levels above the player.
- Early Warlock spell milestones including Fear at 5 and specialization at 10.
- Different class combat tips, campaign stages (1-10 / 10-70 / 70-80 / 80-90 / 90).
- The Leveling tab's map and coach show the current zone instead of an unrelated mount.
- What's My Best Move? opens an available chosen quest or your in-game map.
- At levels 1-9, the Dashboard recommends leveling before unrelated mount goals.

HOW TO USE
Open /ghnav > Leveling. Read DO NEXT, ROUTE, class combat and milestone.
Use WHAT'S MY BEST MOVE?, OPEN QUEST LOG, or FIND GEAR.
No automatic playing or quest automation. Quest log/map data comes from WoW.
The guidance is not a timed fastest-route comparison; a full waypoint quest database is not included.

Preserved: original Gear Hunter screen, mount guides, MapHub data and all eight approved discovery icons.
