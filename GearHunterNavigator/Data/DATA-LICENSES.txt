MAPHUB 0.8.0 - WORLD RARE DATABASE REPORT

Bundle: 1702 named rare entries across 105 zone datasets, 3280 spawn positions.
Additional bundled records from earlier MapHub ExpandedMapData.lua: Legion and Shadowlands rares/treasures.
These figures count mapped spawn positions, not all unique NPCs in World of Warcraft.
This DOES NOT guarantee every rare, every phase, every expansion or every zone has been cataloged.
No map images or extra updates needed: WoW renders its own map and MapHub overlays data.
All locations load immediately when their map is selected; a live rare need not be visible or active.
There are no CMD/PS1/PowerShell files in this addon ZIP.

What's new: 105 bundled old-world/new-expansion zone datasets, compact label deduplication for multiple spawn points, red rare skulls retained, new /mhworld diagnostics.

Data source: MIT-licensed AllTheThings project (specific outdoor-zone Rares.lua coordinates).
Some locations are historic, phased, or subject to patch changes.
Do not mistake a documented possible spawn point for an enemy that is currently spawned.
Real in-client testing and full-world audit remain pending.
